Fair questions

Is this affiliated with Google?

No. Panic Later is an independent software maker. The extension audits information you enter — and the visible text of Maps pages you open — and never communicates with Google on your behalf. Google Business Profile and Google Maps are trademarks of Google LLC.

Does it see or store my Google password or profile?

No. It never asks for credentials, never logs into anything, and never sends data anywhere. Everything it knows stays in your browser's local storage.

Why one-time payment instead of a subscription?

Because the audit fixes a problem — it shouldn't rent you relief. One $29 payment, all Pro features, forever on this device.

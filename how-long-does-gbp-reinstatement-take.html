How long does Google Business Profile reinstatement take?

Honest answer: it depends less on Google and more on how clean your first appeal is. Here are the realistic ranges and what moves them.

What actually adds weeks

  • Multiple open appeals. Every new submission resets the queue. One at a time, always.
  • Appealing before fixing. Reviewers check the live profile. "We fixed it" while the stuffed name is still visible = instant template rejection.
  • Changing the business name/address mid-appeal. Looks like evasion; triggers deeper review.
  • Creating a duplicate profile. Both eventually get removed.

What shortens it

  • A first appeal that already answers the likely rejection: compliance changes listed, days-suspended stated, evidence menu offered.
  • Character-identical NAP across your website and top directories before you submit.
  • A dated case log — reviewers and escalation teams both respond to a clean timeline.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026 · Panic Later (independent software)

1. The short version

We don't collect your data. Our products run entirely on your device, and nothing you do inside them is transmitted to us or to anyone else.

2. What our software stores, and where

  • Audit answers and generated reports stay in your browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local) on your device. They never leave it.
  • Your license key, after activation, is stored the same way — locally, on your device, for offline verification.
  • Case journal entries you write are stored locally on your device only.

3. The Maps Radar feature

Our Chrome extension includes a detection feature that reads the visible text of the Google Maps page you have open in your own browser, looking for suspension notices. The resulting snapshot (page address, business name, whether a suspension notice was detected) is stored locally on your device for up to 30 minutes and is used only to display information back to you. It is not sent anywhere.

4. What we never do

  • We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, cookies, or advertising.
  • We do not require accounts. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • We do not include remote code. All code ships inside the extension package.

5. Payments

Purchases are processed by our merchant of record, Lemon Squeezy, LLC. When you buy, Lemon Squeezy handles your payment details under their privacy policy. We never see your card details — we receive only a license key record for activation.

6. Children's privacy

Our products are general-audience tools and do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date. Given that our entire policy is "we don't collect," we don't expect drama here.
